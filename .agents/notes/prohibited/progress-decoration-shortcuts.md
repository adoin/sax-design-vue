# Progress decoration shortcuts

The user rejected striped and grid textures. Do not reintroduce them as Progress texture values or examples.

Sparse large glossy circles do not match the referenced popup.png microbubble field. Preserve dense irregular small particles and the repeated-image overlay principle.

Star sparkle must change individual brightness/size/rays rather than only translate the whole pattern. Sea waves must lift, curl and fall rather than only slide a sine line. Landing must spread into water rather than retain a backward-facing hook. Adjacent crest/trough phases must differ, with continuous motion through the cycle.

Do not require a tall progress bar for the authored texture proportions: use actual-height scaling in both axes.
